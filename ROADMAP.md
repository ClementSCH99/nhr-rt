# Future roadmap

The detailed roadmap for the next expansion phase will be defined separately
after the v0.2.0 refactoring and final hardware validation.

The expected themes are:

- complete workflow control through the 64-bit client;
- external fail-closed interlocks, including CAN/BMS signals;
- a supervised graphical interface;
- richer logging, metadata and long-duration evidence management;
- deployment, configuration and operational monitoring improvements.

Until that roadmap is reviewed, these themes are directions rather than
committed interfaces or milestones. The current release remains focused on a
safe local service, reusable workflows and traceable execution evidence.

The completed development history is indexed in
[the validation record](docs/VALIDATION.md) and [archive registry](archives/README.md).
